Urban Issue Response UK

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower cities and communities with data-driven insights and innovative solutions to address urban challenges, fostering sustainable development and improving the quality of life for all residents. We aim to use extensive collaboration with the public to identify the issues making greatest impacts on their day to day lives and through the use of local buisnesses, charities and governments, provide the most effective long term solutions.

Some of the issues we aim to solve include:

  • Unsafe Spaces
  • Homelessness
  • Inability to Access Healthcare
  • Inability to Access Food
  • Inadequate Public Infrastructure
  • Lack of Public Services

Who We Work With

We work with a wide range of stakeholders including local governments, community organizations, businesses, and residents to co-create solutions that are tailored to the unique needs of each community. By fostering collaboration and leveraging data-driven insights, we strive to create sustainable and impactful change in urban environments.

Components

This website has been set up to allow users to easily access our wide range of resources and data related to urban issues and solutions being put in place in the different towns and cities we are currently working in across the UK.
Its aim is to provide information to the public about issues reported in their area, the short term and long term reliefy we have organised, as well as the policies we are pushing in their local governments to irradicate the probems.
Additionally, it provides a tool for the public to report issues in their area, and for local buisnesses and charities to sign up to be part of the solution.

How You Can Help

Report issues you find yourself or your community facing!!!

These issues can included:

  • A rise in homelessness and rough sleeping
  • Unsafe spaces or infrastructure
  • Inability to access clean water
  • Difficulty accessing food
  • Unsafe living conditions
  • Lack of Public Services or the inadequate condition of such services
  • Inability to access essential services

To report an issue, click here Report Issue or navigate to the Issues page and fill out the form with as much detail as possible. Your report will be reviewed by our team and used to inform our efforts to address urban challenges in your community.
You will be required to sign into an account before submitting your report.

You can also help by sharing our website and resources with your friends, family, and community members to raise awareness about the issues we are working to solve and the solutions we are implementing. Together, we can make a difference in creating more sustainable and livable spaces for everyone.

If you are a local business or charity interested in partnering with us to provide solutions to urban challenges, please reach out to us through our contact details at the bottom of the page. We are always looking for new partners to help us create positive change in communities across the UK.

How We Can Help

We can help your community by providing the solutions like:

  • The organisation and funding of shelters and food banks
  • Pushing the installation of cameras and better lighting in unsafe areas to discourage anti-social behaviour
  • The removal of hazardous structures and materials and the improvement of infrastructure
  • The improved supply of crucial resources such as heating and clean water
  • Aid in setting up better funding for public services
  • Pushing policies in local governments to address urban challenges
  • Putting pressure on local authorities to implement more adequate or improved accessibility to essential services

But we cannot do it alone. We need your help to make a difference in creating more sustainable and livable spaces for everyone.
